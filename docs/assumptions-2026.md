# Verified Assumptions — India, August 2026

Researched from public sources as of **August 2026**. Rates and rules change and
the new **Income-tax Act 2025** (in force 1 Apr 2026) may re-number sections —
**verify against current notifications before acting.** Not investment advice.

## Fixed-income & small-savings schemes

Small-savings rates were held **unchanged for Jul–Sep 2026 (Q2 FY2026-27)** — the
9th consecutive quarter flat (DEA, Ministry of Finance, 30 Jun 2026).

| Scheme | Rate | Limit | Eligibility / notes |
|--------|------|-------|---------------------|
| **SCSS** (Senior Citizen Savings) | **8.2%** quarterly | ₹30 L / person | 60+ (or 55–60 within 1 mo of VRS/superannuation; defence 50+). 5-yr lock, +3-yr extend. |
| **POMIS** (Post Office MIS) | **7.4%** monthly | ₹9 L single / ₹15 L joint | 5-yr tenure. |
| **RBI Floating Rate Savings Bond 2020** | **8.05%** half-yearly | no cap | NSC + 0.35%; resets 6-monthly; 7-yr lock. Senior premature exit: 60–70 → 4 yr, 70–80 → 5 yr, 80+ → 6 yr. |
| **Senior bank FD** | ~7.0% (up to ~7.75% private) | no cap | +0.25–0.75% over base; 80+ get top slabs at some banks. |
| **Small Finance Bank FD** | up to ~8.3–8.6% | — | ⚠️ **DICGC insures only ₹5 L per bank** — capital risk. |

**Closed to new money:** Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (ended Mar 2025) and
**PMVVY** (ended Mar 2023). Government's designated replacement for both: **SCSS**.

## Taxation — FY2026-27 (AY 2027-28)

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| **LTCG, equity/equity MF** | **12.5%** over **₹1.25 L/yr** exempt; long-term = held > 12 months. |
| **STCG, equity** | **20%**. |
| **Debt MF** (units bought on/after 1 Apr 2023) | Taxed at **slab**, no indexation, regardless of holding period. |
| **Gold ETF/fund** (bought on/after 1 Apr 2025) | **12.5% LTCG** after > 12 months. |
| **Multi-asset / hybrid** | ≥65% equity → equity-taxed; 35–65% → 12.5% after 24 mo; <35% → slab. |
| **§80TTB** (₹50k senior interest deduction) | **OLD regime only.** |
| **New Regime (default)** | Income up to **₹12 L nil** (₹60k §87A rebate) ≈ ₹12.75 L with ₹75k standard deduction — **per person**. No age concession. Rebate does **not** apply to LTCG/STCG. |
| **Old Regime senior exemption** | ₹3 L (60–80), ₹5 L (80+); §80TTB + deductions available. |
| **TDS on interest (§194A)** | Senior threshold **₹1 L/bank**; Form 15H to stop TDS if liability nil. |
| **Advance tax (§207)** | Not payable by a resident senior with no business income. |

**Strategic upshot:** for an interest-heavy retiree, the **New Regime's ₹12 L nil
band per person** usually beats the Old Regime's ₹3 L + §80TTB. Split income across
both spouses to use two nil bands; the ₹12 L/yr need largely fits inside them.
Confirm old-vs-new with a CA each year.

## Gaps & the 4th bucket

- **Medical inflation ~12–14%** (≈2× general) — costs roughly double every 5 years.
  Needs a **dedicated ₹15–25 L medical corpus** + base floater + super top-up,
  opened *now* (entry-age windows tighten after ~65; senior co-pay 10–30%; PED wait
  up to 48 months).
- **Emergency fund:** 12–24 months of expenses (₹12–24 L), ring-fenced, separate.
- **Longevity:** joint-life, return-of-purchase-price annuity (LIC Jeevan Akshay
  ~7.5%, or NPS) for essentials — **nominal**, laddered at 60/65/70.
- **Sovereign Gold Bonds discontinued** (no new tranche since Feb 2024) → gold ETFs.
- **REITs/InvITs** ~7–9%, quarterly, with contractual rent escalations — small sleeve.
- **Estate:** a nominee is a custodian, not the heir — make a **registered Will**,
  add "Either or Survivor" holdings, refresh nominations.
- **Survivor risk:** the 4-year age gap means the younger spouse may manage alone
  6+ years — joint-life annuities only; keep the portfolio operable by the survivor.

### The 4-bucket structure (on a ₹2.5 Cr income corpus)

| Bucket | Purpose | ~Size | Contents |
|--------|---------|-------|----------|
| 1 · Safety | Monthly income + lifelong floor | ₹85 L | SCSS, RBI FRSB, POMIS, liquid/arbitrage, annuity floor |
| 2 · Preserve | Refill Bucket 1 | ₹65 L | Banking & PSU debt, target-maturity/G-Sec, multi-asset, REIT sleeve |
| 3 · Grow | Beat inflation, fund step-ups | ₹100 L | Nifty 50 + Next 50 index, flexi-cap, large & mid-cap, gold ETF |
| 4 · Protect (ring-fenced, **+₹40 L on top**) | Health + emergencies | ₹40 L | ₹25 L medical corpus + ₹15 L contingency (liquid) |
